## The Moment Around The Fix

When someone asks for technical help, they are often already interrupted. The machine is not doing what they need, and
their day has lost momentum.

That means the way a fix is handled matters. Clear questions, quiet confidence, and good notes can make the whole
experience less frustrating.

## What Calm Looks Like

Calm does not mean slow. It means the work has enough structure that nobody has to guess what is happening.

- name the next check
- explain risk before changing something
- avoid making the user feel blamed
- write down the result

That is not decoration. It is part of good engineering practice.
