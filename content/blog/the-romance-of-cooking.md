In a sense, cooking is really just like romance. You take ingredients that are completely different with each other, have different ingredients and origins, and some how, making it work into a perfectly combined dish. That is why a common metaphor for cooking is "marrying", like making pasta where you marries the sauce and the pasta together cohesively and turns it into something way more beautiful than the ingredients by themselves could ever achieve. A relationship is the same, two people, who some how meet each other in this wide world, like two ingredients imported from different side of the world ended up in the same kitchen, they started talking, then know more about each other, became each other's friend then garnished with a marriage.

 In a relationship of course, there are a lot of things that can go wrong, maybe their ideals are not aligning, maybe the timing is not right. I find it quite cute that in the kitchen we also suffer the same problems in our journey to create a dish, one cannot combine bitter melon and cherry in a pie for example, or if the timings is not right your tiramisu might ended up a pile of mush. Understanding individually how different ingredients tastes is key to know what to add into a dish, similarly, one must know themselves before finding their perfect other.

Ultimately, love, relationships are just cooking sessions where we could get lucky and make a perfect dish right off the bat, or we could suffered through countless burnt pies in order to create a perfect one. The key thing is to not stop, learn what you can change or avoid in future and move on.

Peace and love,

Harry
