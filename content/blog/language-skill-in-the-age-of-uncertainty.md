You have probably came across some articles about how kids lately heavily rely on AI for their writing and reading, effectively outsourcing their brain to Artificial Intelligence models. It is a worrisome sign of what kind of future we are heading to, for me, I think it is an opportunity for us - the last generation that have an AI-free childhood - to thrive and take the right steps to protect our own intelligence as a species. I strongly believe that the skills that we should prioritize in any language is writing and reading in order to thrive and grow as an individual. 

I have a wonderful opportunity to work with the son of my Professor to guide him through his prep for the IELTS exam he needs for his university exam. Usually we learn techniques, requirements and basics, however, once a month, after a mock test for the speaking session, we does a "Discussion section" where I give a question and we together find an answer for it. Of course I have my own answer already in my head but it is really interesting to hear what does the youth thinks about the same question.  The question was "Is English a Subject at school?". After quite an eventful discussion we came up with a wonderful distinction: "English is a Language not an subject". Interestingly, we also noticed that not all skills of a language is the same, for instance, we learn speaking and listening naturally by copying our parents and the people we interact with, we are Natural Language Learners! Conversely, we need someone else to teach us how to communicate those language indirectly, I.E writing and reading.

The question remains, when we outsource the skills that we cannot learn naturally, who will be the one teaching us those skill in the future? 

That brings us to our final point, our future. When we learn speaking and listening, we learn by copying and regurgitate what we hear into our own speak. Likewise, the only way for us to learn writing is through writing and formulate those patterns and ideas we read and hear into our own paragraphs. Hence, in order to rise above and be an intelligence person, we must preserve the art of writing and the skill of reading. So write, start a blog, buy a paper journal, physicalize your ideas into writing. Be a better person, a more intelligent person, one paragraph at a time.

-Harry
