## Context

This university project focused on a web application for displaying and visualizing academic data. I served as project manager from February to June 2024, leading a team of nine during an 11-week development and deployment effort.

## My Role

My responsibilities centered on the project as a whole: coordinating people, technical direction, documentation, and stakeholder communication.

- Led the team through development and deployment.
- Coordinated meetings and the project's technical documentation.
- Oversaw the technology choices and overall project direction.
- Conducted feasibility studies and risk assessments.
- Worked with stakeholders throughout the project.

## Delivery

The team developed and deployed the academic-data web application. My contribution was leading and coordinating that work; the implementation was a team effort.

## What This Work Shows

This project is an example of my experience working across technical planning and team leadership. It required keeping the project's direction understandable to both the team and its stakeholders.
